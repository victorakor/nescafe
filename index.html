01 — THE SACHET

Born in 1938

Nestlé launched Nescafé in Switzerland after years of research into keeping coffee fresh. One sachet of soluble coffee started a habit that went around the world.

02 — THE BREW

Brewed, then dried

Nescafé starts with roasted coffee beans that are brewed and dried into soluble granules, so water turns them into coffee in seconds.

03 — THE ICE

Pour over ice

Nescafé works as well cold as hot. The granules dissolve fast, so the ice stays cold and the coffee stays strong.

04 — THE SERVE

Your cup is ready

Nescafé is made for everyday moments: a slow morning, a study break, a long afternoon. Pick yours up.
